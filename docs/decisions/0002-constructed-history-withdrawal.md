# Withdraw constructed historical records at every catalog boundary

Status: accepted for implementation; public deployment pending.

The historical workbench generator assigns official edition identities to four
hand-constructed Title 18 provision versions. Independently retained OLRC bytes
contradict the published section1001 table and175b reference. A source digest
proves byte identity, not publisher authorship. Existing cached catalogs and
independent topic literals can republish those claims even after a generator fix.
The code-generated Public Law, CFR and case snippets also lack retained publisher
acquisition evidence; their exact artifact identities and digests are withdrawn
from accepted evidence without asserting that the underlying authorities are false.

A single source-integrity withdrawal policy removes the known constructed
producer identities and their dependent evidence, claims and assertions from
ordinary catalog ingestion, replay, query and export. It is an acceptance overlay,
not a rewrite of old Git tags, immutable upstream bytes or publication history.
The six unrelated, explicitly legacy-derived sections in each edition remain
available with their original derivation label. Required coverage becomes partial
with counted unavailable provisions; absence never becomes an unchanged-law claim.

The same policy gates independently generated topic members, authorities and
amendment events. A source-correction notice identifies the withdrawn editions,
reason, prior publication and genuine source fallback. Historical body, search,
relationship inspector, topic feeds and analytical exports must agree. Current
source-backed corpus records are not globally denied by citation. Previously
constructed version identities cannot become official merely by loading a cache
or changing its asserted review flags; a genuine replacement needs independently
retained source evidence and a new accepted version/publication.

Acceptance uses the public catalog/query/export interfaces and generated outputs.
A cached record, dependent assertion or independent topic literal must not leak
withdrawn content. Positive controls retain the six legacy sections and unrelated
editions. Old tags and raw source bytes remain addressable. Preview and live
publication receipts are recorded separately; code tests do not establish a
public withdrawal or source-text repair.

Generated neighborhood URLs for withdrawn versions receive explicit source-gap
records, replacing stale derivative bytes without deleting retained source objects.
The historical-search manifest carries the same correction metadata. Database
lexical reads apply the withdrawal before limiting results, including databases
populated before this policy. Static affected pages disclose the historical gap
and link official sources and prior publication records even without JavaScript.
